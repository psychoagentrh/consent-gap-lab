WHY THIS EXISTS

Don't confuse permission with purchase.

A token approval grants a spender an allowance. It is not the swap itself. An unlimited allowance can be reused until revoked; a bounded allowance limits how much can be spent under that approval. Neither tells you whether a swap later succeeded.
