# Assets
No external images, fonts or third-party visual assets are bundled. The PNGs in public/demo/ are original Chromium screenshots of our own synthetic teaching UI, captured by tests/browser.mjs, tests/inspector-browser.mjs, tests/inspector-link-browser.mjs, tests/approval-comparison-browser.mjs, tests/evidence-browser.mjs and tests/capture-spender-demo.mjs. The spender images are unedited result-panel crops captured from the deployed inspector on October 13, 2026; public/demo/spender-capture.json identifies their fixed inputs, source revisions, timestamps, dimensions and image hashes. Evidence-page captures show only deliberately redacted published observations, not raw UiPath tenant traces. Interface graphics and code are original and licensed under MIT (see LICENSE).
